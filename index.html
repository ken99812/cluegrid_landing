Everything in one grid

A daily puzzle

The same new puzzle for everyone in your language, every day. Keep your streak going.

Leaderboards

Today, this week and all time, with everyone or just your friends. Add friends with a short code.

Coins for every word

Each word you get right pays coins. Spend them on a hint, a letter or a whole word when you're stuck.

New puzzles all the time

Hundreds of puzzles in every language, mixed from Easy to Expert, with more arriving every day.

Plays offline

Puzzles download to your phone. Solve on the train; your progress syncs when you're back online.

Spot a mistake?

Tap Report an error on any puzzle. Every report is checked, and wrong clues are fixed quickly.
